[Yafra](/yafra) didn't get much attention this year for different reasons.
From my point of view, it is almost feature complete.
I currently own three analog cameras, two of them without interchangeable lenses.
<!-- more --> 
A missing feature, lens information, therefore never had a hight priority for me. And, as [mentioned previously]({{< ref "retro2024.md" >}}), I can't devote much time to this side project anymore.
So I was seriously considering to cancel my paid developer account and publish the source code, effectively sunsetting the app. By chance, I read the only review Yafra got this year, and it went like this `Never ever change this app its so f-ing perfect. ❤️`. So I guess, I will add another €99,- to Apple's revenue and perhaps update Yafra next in 2026 or finally get [Yafra Tagger]({{< ref "yafra-tagger.md" >}}) ready for the Mac App Store.
